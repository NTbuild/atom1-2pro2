# atom1-2pro2 — Loader study

Two loading animations that turn into a site's hero, in one HTML file.

- **01 Deal** — cards are dealt from a deck one by one: each is tossed, flips over and lands in an arc with a recoil. The full hand breathes, a sky arrives, and the arc unfolds into a tilted 3D carousel inside a rounded hero window.
- **02 Ring** — cards lay into a tumbling 3D ring one after another, then the camera dives inside and the ring becomes a curved gallery behind the hero.

Open `index.html` in any browser, no build step. Switch loaders with the pill at the top (or keys `1` / `2`, `R` to replay).
Motion runs on damped springs; the card thumbnails, card back and sky are all drawn in the browser at load. Fonts load from Google Fonts.
With reduced motion turned on, the page goes straight to the hero.

The loader concepts were inspired by a reel from [@avi_vashishta29](https://www.instagram.com/avi_vashishta29/); all code, images and copy here are original.
